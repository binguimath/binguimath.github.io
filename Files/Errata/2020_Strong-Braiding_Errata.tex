\title{Errata to ``Unbounded field operators in categorical extensions of conformal nets"}
\author{Bin Gui}


\begin{document}\sloppy

\maketitle


\begin{enumerate}
\item In Sec. 2.7, Def. 2.42, there is a typo: $\wtd I_2$ should be clockwise (rather than anticlockwise) to $\wtd I_1$. Also, the second paragraph can be more precise. The revised version of Def. 2.42 (with only one paragraph) is as follows.

Let $\mathscr{E}_{\mathrm{par}}^w$ be a categorical partial extension. Given
$\mathcal{H}_{i_1},\ldots,\mathcal{H}_{i_n} \in \mathcal{F}$,
$\widetilde{I} \in \widetilde{\mathcal{J}}$, 
we define $\mathcal{H}_{i_n,\ldots,i_1}(\widetilde{I})$ to be the set
of all
\[
(\fk a_n,\ldots,\fk a_1)
\in
\fk{H}_{i_n}(\widetilde I_n)
\times \cdots \times
\fk{H}_{i_1}(\widetilde I_1)
\]
such that $\wtd I_t\subset\wtd I$ and $\fk a_t\in\fk H_{i_t}(\wtd I_t)$ for each $1\leq t\leq n$, and $\wtd I_s$ is clockwise to $\wtd I_t$ whenever $1\leq s<t\leq n$.


\item In the last paragraph of the proof of Thm. 3.39, the formula $\dim_{\mc C_V}(U)=\dim_{\Rep^0(A)}(U)$ is not correct, and needs to be deleted. In the subsequent sentence, the formula
\begin{align*}
D(\mc C_V)/D(\Rep^0(A))=\dim_{\Rep^0(A)}(U)
\end{align*}
citing [KO02] Thm. 4.5 is also citing incorrectly. The correct formula in that paper is
\begin{align*}
D(\mc C_V)/D(\Rep^0(A))=\dim_{\mc C_V}(U)
\end{align*}
These two mistakes indeed cancel each other, which again implies
$D(\mc C_V)/D(\mc C_U)=\dim_{\mc C_V}(U)$ and hence finishing the proof of Thm. 3.39.


\item In the proof of Lem. 3.47, the last sentence ``Thus they also hold for $V_\Lambda\otimes V_L$ and hence for $V_\Lambda$ by theorems 3.38 and 3.39'' is a little misleading. The more appropriate one should be ``Thus they also hold for $V_\Lambda\otimes V_L$ (by theorems 3.38 and 3.39) and hence for $V_\Lambda$ ''.
\end{enumerate}
